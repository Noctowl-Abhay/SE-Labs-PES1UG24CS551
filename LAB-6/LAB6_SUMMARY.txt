-------------------------------------------------
OBSERVATIONS
-------------------------------------------------
- Line coverage alone is easy to over-trust. Branches such as the
  "miles > 300" check and the "driver_age < 21" check need both a True
  and a False case, not just one pass through the line.
- The early `break` for drivers under 21 on long trips needed its own
  test, since it skips the rest of the trip segments.
- The telematics streak counter only resets on a slow reading, so a
  test with a slow reading between two fast ones was needed to cover
  the else branch.
- Edge values worth noting: exactly 100 mph does not count as speeding
  (> 100), and exactly 300 miles uses the normal 0.20/mile rate.
